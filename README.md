## About

I'm a Computer Science graduate student at **Texas A&M University** (GPA 4.0/4.0) and a former **Software Development Engineer at Zscaler**. I work at the boundary of **security, systems, and AI**: post-quantum cryptography, malware analysis, agentic-AI / Model Context Protocol (MCP) security, and secure software supply chains.

I research under **Dr. Marcus Botacin's Lab** (streaming algorithms for malware analysis) and collaborate with the **GAIA group** (transfer-learning pipelines on HPC clusters). Earlier, hardware-security research at NIT Durgapur led to an **IEEE publication**.
